| B19 | ❌ Open  | **`[RES]` snapshot state is computed in code/emit order, not chronological order.** `resourceSnapshot()` mutates shared closure state (`lastSnapshotTime`, `prevReadyNamesState`, `prevOnCDNamesState`) at call time, but timeline sections execute in source order (the DEATH block runs before the `[YOU] [CD]` loop) while their event times are non-monotonic. Effects: (a) the 2.0s debounce false-suppresses the `[RES]` line on the first owner-CD cast processed after a death — defeating F160's "RES on every major defensive"; (b) forced-full death/CD snapshots overwrite `prevReadyNamesState` at a chronologically-later time, so a later *delta* `[RES]` diffs against the wrong baseline → bogus "newly ready / newly on CD" lines fed to the model; (c) deaths <2s apart (and friendly-then-enemy adjacency) silently lose their death `[RES]`.
