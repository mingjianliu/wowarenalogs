| B14 | ❌ Open  | **Critical — non-English locale spell-name leak.** On non-English clients the timeline mixes languages: `<player_loadout>`/`[CD]`/enemy-CD/kill-window lines resolve to English (by spell-ID), but `[CAST]` filler casts, `[CLEANSE]`/`[DISPEL]`, `Offensive Dispel Summary`, `[ABSORBED:]`, and the `[DEATH]` top-damage breakdown render the raw localized name. Root cause: `getEnglishSpellName` is backed by the CD-only `spellEffects.json`, so non-CD heals (e.g. cast IDs 85673 Word of Glory, 360995 Verdant Embrace) miss → localized fallback at `matchTimeline.ts:755`. Meta-eval of zh-CN corpus (2026-06-13, full 224-match pass): leak present in **224/224 prompts** — 310 distinct tokens, **13,982 occurrences**. **Player-facing severity confirmed at full corpus: raw Chinese leaked into the coaching the player reads in 132/224 responses (58%)** — rate climbed 39% (n=83) → 52% (n=155) → 58% (n=224) as Evoker/Druid/Monk/Paladin (heavy non-CD filler casts) were added. Two failure modes: model **drops** the unmappable casts entirely (008: "did not build any finding on a spell I could not confidently map"), or **leaks Chinese** into findings (013: "keep feeding 真言术：盾, 苦修…"; 014/010/025/028 similar). Per-spec leak severity (avg CJK chars/prompt): **Holy Paladin 312 (worst) > Resto Shaman 269 > Pres Evoker 252 > Disc 226 > MW Monk 192 > Holy Priest 159 > Resto Druid 129** — melee-adjacent healers leak most. Fix: back `getEnglishSpellName` with a complete spell-ID→English map (extend mined dataset / fold in `spellIdLists.json`). | `matchTimeline.ts`, `packages/shared/src/data/spellEffectData.ts`, `packages/shared/src/data/spellEffects.json`              |
| B19 | ❌ Open  | **`[RES]` snapshot state is computed in code/emit order, not chronological order.** `resourceSnapshot()` mutates shared closure state (`lastSnapshotTime`, `prevReadyNamesState`, `prevOnCDNamesState`) at call time, but timeline sections execute in source order (the DEATH block runs before the `[YOU] [CD]` loop) while their event times are non-monotonic. Effects: (a) the 2.0s debounce false-suppresses the `[RES]` line on the first owner-CD cast processed after a death — defeating F160's "RES on every major defensive"; (b) forced-full death/CD snapshots overwrite `prevReadyNamesState` at a chronologically-later time, so a later *delta* `[RES]` diffs against the wrong baseline → bogus "newly ready / newly on CD" lines fed to the model; (c) deaths <2s apart (and friendly-then-enemy adjacency) silently lose their death `[RES]`. Fix: gather snapshot requests as `(time, forceFull)`, sort chronologically, then compute debounce/delta — don't rely on execution order. | `matchTimeline.ts`, `resourceSnapshot.ts` |
