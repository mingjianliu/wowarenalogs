# F193 V2 (contested-trade facts + EV rubric) — A/B Comparison Report

**Change tested:** F193 healer offense V2 — `[CONTESTED]` fact tier (team 70–85% band, CC ready at Full DR
on enemy healer, no active enemy CDs, enemyInterruptsReady cast-risk context) + rubric permitting
trade findings ONLY with a `[CONTESTED]` anchor at ≤Medium confidence (commit `e75b6666` vs `d33f2d61`).
**Target dimensions:** accuracy / labelBias (failure mode = false criticism of legitimate defensive play).
**Design:** 18 pairs — 12 contested-stratified + 6 identical-prompt negative controls (rubric-only delta);
responders role-played each arm's full system prompt (Sonnet), scored by 36 isolated blind judges
(Sonnet, one per item, mapping.json untouched until `abCompareStats`).

## Regressions

None CI-confirmed on decision-weight dimensions. noise\* excluded per workflow rule (deterministic
measure contradicts it). outcomeAlignment/focusCalibration point estimates are slightly negative but
inconclusive — monitor; note the judges repeatedly dinged BOTH arms for "doesn't explain why the
match was won", a pre-existing trait unrelated to F193.

## New issues found (both arms / pre-existing, filed for triage)

1. **windowContribution "no owner CC observed" contradiction** — both arms' responders independently
   caught the `<healer_offense>` kill-window line claiming no CC while the timeline shows a Psychic
   Scream cast at the window's start second (ordinal 001) and interrupted Sleep Walk attempts counted
   as "no CC" (ordinal 012). Likely boundary/attempt-counting defects in `computeWindowContributions`.
2. **[SLACK] zero-output contradiction** — a slack segment claiming zero damage while the timeline
   shows casts inside it (ordinal 012 treatment).
3. **[CD] cast-annotation HP% vs adjacent [STATE] mismatch** (ordinal 003/005 judges verified it in
   the prompt text).

## Decision

- **accuracy / labelBias (target): no regression** (Δ 0.00 / −0.11, CIs straddle 0).
- **Safety contract: deterministically verified** — 100% anchor compliance, confidence caps held,
  hard 70% floor held, negative controls clean.
- **Recommendation: ADOPT** — keep `V2_CONTESTED_TRADES: true`. The rubric change behaves exactly as
  designed at the behavior level; judged quality shows no CI-confirmed cost on any decision-weight
  dimension. (The three pre-existing data contradictions above are filed as follow-ups, not blockers.)
