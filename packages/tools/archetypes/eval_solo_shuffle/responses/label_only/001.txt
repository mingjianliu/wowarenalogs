This round fits the cc_tunnel_reactive pattern clearly: the Mistweaver Monk spent most of 1:53 reacting to repeated CC chains on herself rather than setting the pace offensively, and the Hunter died despite full HP at T-10 because the healer had no major tools left in the final burst window. The archetype's hallmark — late CD usage and high missed-cleanse rate — showed up in the death sequence: both the Hunter's Exhilaration charges and Aspect of the Turtle were already spent by the time the fatal 0.81M spike hit at 1:43, and the Monk had trinket on a 35-second cooldown during the simultaneous Spider Venom and Scorpid Venom at 1:45–1:48.

The enemy team executed a textbook cc_then_burst pattern. They used Dragon's Breath on the Monk at 0:20, Hammer of Justice on the Hunter at 0:24, and immediately fired Combustion at 0:28 — driving the Hunter to 26% at 0:36. The Monk's Life Cocoon at 0:37 saved the Hunter at 25%, which was a correct reactive call, but it meant that a 120-second CD was on cooldown for the rest of the round. The Monk's trinket burned at 0:50 on an Intimidation, leaving it unavailable for the Dragon's Breath at 1:08 when the Warrior had Avatar active and the Fire Mage was at 15% — the team's clearest offensive opportunity.

The two moments where proactive play could have changed the outcome were at 1:06–1:08 and 1:22–1:26. At 1:08, the Fire Mage was at 15% HP, Bestial Wrath was active on the Hunter, and Avatar had been running since 0:44. A Paralysis on the Holy Paladin at this moment could have forced a kill before Avenging Wrath came online at 1:36. Instead, no CC went out on the enemy healer until the Leg Sweep at 1:22, which landed on the Marksmanship Hunter rather than the Paladin — less threatening given the Paladin was still at 100% health and had Avenging Wrath 14 seconds away. The second Combustion at 1:48 with the Monk double-poisoned and trinket-less completed the conversion.

At 31% dampening and with all defensive CDs spent, the Monk could not outheal a Rapid Fire (208k) plus Fireball (172k) combination in the final three seconds. The correctable lesson is to identify the enemy healer-interrupt window earlier — around 1:06 when the enemy healer was briefly under pressure — rather than waiting to spend CC reactively on damage dealers after the enemy healer has already stabilized the round.
