The Power Infusion cast at 0:48 came during a period of relative calm (shaman at 66%) just before the second Recklessness window. In heavy_burst_tunnel rounds, Power Infusion's primary value is boosting your own healing throughput during burst windows. Casting it 1 second before Recklessness fired (0:49) means the throughput bonus arrived as damage ramped — likely correct, though a slightly earlier cast as the first signs of pressure appeared would have been cleaner.

The Psychic Scream at 1:19 hitting the enemy Disc Priest (player 5) was the match's best offensive play: the enemy warrior was at 33% by 1:17, and silencing the enemy healer while pursuing a kill is exactly the right instinct. The missed kill was the determining factor — if the warrior had died in the 1:15–1:25 window, the round ends there rather than at 1:50.

The core lesson for heavy_burst_tunnel against Fury Warrior: you need your team to finish the enemy warrior, or you will run out of Pain Suppression before they run out of Recklessness. At 1:30 the warrior was at 12% with Psychic Scream on the enemy healer. Using the Psychic Scream on the warrior in that moment rather than the healer — extending their inability to use defensive cooldowns while the hunter's Bestial Wrath was still active — might have secured the kill. Offensive CC on a sub-15% warrior is worth more at this match stage than CC on their healer, because eliminating the warrior ends the pressure entirely rather than briefly reducing incoming healing.
