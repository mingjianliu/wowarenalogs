Match 025 is a heavy_burst_tunnel loss — 1:50 at 31% dampening — against a Fury Warrior who used Recklessness and Avatar three times and whose third window killed the Enhancement Shaman with Rampage, Execute, and Bloodthirst doing the majority of lethal damage. The archetype shows 74% offensive participation and a mean of 3.2 CC offensive actions per match for winning Discipline Priests in heavy_burst_tunnel rounds. Here, the priest used Psychic Scream once at 1:19 and only on the enemy Disc Priest — not on the Fury Warrior doing the damage. This discrepancy is the central mechanical problem in the match.

The first burst window at 0:09 through 0:34 produced a 0.72M spike on the Enhancement Shaman. Pain Suppression came at 0:22 — 13 seconds after the window opened and after the shaman had already recovered to 100% at 0:18 from the initial burst. The suppression still covered ongoing pressure from Recklessness which was still active, so it wasn't wasted, but the optimal deployment would have been at 0:12 when the shaman was at 57% and still taking damage at the burst peak. Power Infusion at 0:48 correctly anticipated the second Recklessness window at 0:49.

The second window at 0:49 through 1:09 produced a 0.59M spike. Pain Suppression came at 0:53 as Hammer of Justice landed on the shaman — this was a good reactive response. Psychic Scream fired at 1:19 on the enemy Disc Priest alone — correct target identification for an offensive CC to shut down healing, but poorly timed relative to the threat state: the enemy Fury Warrior was at 73% and was not actively pressing a kill at that moment. Using Psychic Scream on the Fury Warrior during the second burst window at 0:49 or on both enemies at 1:15 when the Fury Warrior was at 43% HP would have created a kill opportunity or scattered the kill threat.

When the third Recklessness and Avatar arrived at 1:31, the friendly priest had no Pain Suppression, no Power Infusion, and no Psychic Scream available. The shaman entered the window at 44% HP and died at 1:50 under 0.77M of sustained Fury Warrior damage. The loss is traceable to CD rationing failure across the first two windows: the 0:22 Pain Suppression was slightly late, leaving one charge that was then spent at 0:53 correctly, but the Psychic Scream was held through both high-danger windows and used at an off-tempo moment. In a heavy_burst_tunnel match with three burst windows, every CC and defensive CD must be tied to a specific window's threat management, not held for a future moment that may not arrive before resources run out.
