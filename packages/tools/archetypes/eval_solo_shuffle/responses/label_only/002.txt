The fast_passive_tunnel archetype defines this round precisely: the enemies locked onto the Arms Warrior immediately and executed a kill before any structured CD rotation could develop. The 35-second duration is near the floor for this archetype, and the Holy Paladin finished the round with every major CD unused — Lay on Hands, Blessing of Protection, Blessing of Spellwarding, Avenging Wrath, and Blessing of Sacrifice never touched the combat.

The enemy opened with Dragon's Breath on the Hunter at 0:17 (removing peel), then drove the Warrior from 97% to dead in roughly 9 seconds under Combustion. The Paladin used Aura Mastery at 0:20 just as Combustion lit — a reasonable read on the incoming magic burst — but the Warrior fell faster than the Paladin's action-to-heal pipeline could respond. The key failure was not identifying the kill window 3–4 seconds earlier and front-loading Blessing of Sacrifice or Blessing of Protection before the Warrior's HP reached critical range.

At 0:29 the Warrior was at 73% HP and the Paladin was at 100% with zero pressure on themselves. This was the moment to spend Blessing of Sacrifice — it would have routed incoming damage to the Paladin, who could absorb it, buying 6–8 seconds of extra effective HP on the Warrior. Instead the Paladin waited. At 0:31 the Warrior was at 55%; Die by the Sword was active; Intimidation landed on the Paladin for 2 seconds. At 0:33 when Freezing Trap hit for 1 second, the Paladin burned their trinket to break it — an expensive response to the cheapest CC in the enemy's toolkit while the Warrior was at 36% and collapsing in real time.

The correct sequence at 0:31–0:33 was: break Intimidation with trinket only if necessary, immediately cast Lay on Hands or Blessing of Protection on the Warrior while the Warrior is above 20% HP, and do not spend the trinket on a 1-second trap while a teammate is dying. In this archetype the round ends before conventional setup develops, which means the Paladin's toolkit needs to fire on the first threatening HP bracket, not after the bracket crosses into lethality.
