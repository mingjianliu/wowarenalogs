The fast_passive_tunnel archetype for Holy Paladins produces 70-second rounds on average where opponents apply steady single-target pressure without structured burst sequences, and 69.2% of tracked cooldowns go unused. This 35-second loss — the shortest match in this set — is an extreme instance of that pattern: the Paladin Dadeluxe never took meaningful damage and ended the round at 100% HP with zero cooldowns deployed, while the Arms Warrior was killed by a 3-ability burst sequence.

The match's structure was dictated by two back-to-back CC windows on the Paladin. Hammer of Justice at 0:24 for 5 seconds removed Dadeluxe during the first damage ramp on the Warrior — the Warrior dropped from 99% to 81% at 0:26 while the Paladin was locked out. When the Hammer expired around 0:29, the Warrior was at 73% and the Fire Mage's Combustion had just activated. That created a 2-second window from roughly 0:29 to 0:31 where the Paladin could act, with Combustion live and the Warrior dropping fast. At 0:31 Intimidation stunned the Paladin again, and a Freezing Trap landed in the same second, burning the PvP trinket. The Warrior then went from 55% to dead in 4 seconds — Fireball 184k, Aimed Shot 181k, Black Arrow 74k — with the Arms Warrior running both Avatar and Die by the Sword simultaneously and still dying.

The window the Paladin had to intervene was narrow but real: approximately 0:29 to 0:31. In those 2 seconds, the Warrior was at 73% and Combustion was active. Lay on Hands — an instant cast that generates a full HP restore — would have pushed the Warrior back to 100% and completely reset the kill attempt. Blessing of Sacrifice, active before the Hammer of Justice landed at 0:24, would have begun siphoning damage from the Warrior to the Paladin during the entire Hammer window, reducing how far the Warrior dropped without the Paladin being present to heal. The challenge is that Blessing of Sacrifice needs to be applied proactively, before the healer is CC'd — which requires reading the kill attempt before it develops, a skill the fast_passive_tunnel archetype demands because the match is often over before a reactive response is possible.

At 0:31, once Intimidation and Freezing Trap consumed the Paladin's trinket, the outcome was determined. The Warrior's own defensives (Avatar active since 0:26, Die by the Sword active since 0:31) could not survive 184k + 181k + 74k in 3 seconds under Combustion against 10% dampening. The Paladin's full toolkit — Lay on Hands, Blessing of Protection, Blessing of Spellwarding, Avenging Wrath, Blessing of Sacrifice, Aura Mastery — was entirely available and entirely unused. The lesson is not that the Paladin made poor decisions during the round; it is that fast_passive_tunnel rounds require pre-emptive Blessing of Sacrifice application before the first healer CC lands, which is the one action that would have changed the arithmetic during the Combustion kill window.
