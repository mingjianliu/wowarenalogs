This passive_swap win stretched to 2:16 and needed 41% dampening to close, which is characteristic of the archetype: low peak burst, multiple target swaps by the enemy, and no single clean kill window — just accumulated pressure until healing becomes insufficient. The Holy Paladin navigated two Combustion cycles and two Bestial Wrath windows competently, and the team deserved this win on endurance.

The round's best sequence was 0:32–0:45: Blessing of Spellwarding on the Marksmanship Hunter at 39% HP created a 6-second invulnerability window, paired with Intimidating Shout at 0:34 on the Mistweaver Monk to interrupt enemy healing simultaneously. The Fire Mage dropped to 42% at 0:45 from the uninterrupted damage during this window. This was the round's clearest kill opportunity — the enemy healer was CC'd, the target was sub-50%, and dampening hadn't started yet. A Hammer of Justice on the Mistweaver Monk when she came out of Intimidating Shout at 0:36 could have extended the lockout and converted a kill. The Paladin had Hammer of Justice available and did not use it.

The second clear opportunity came at 1:37 when the Fire Mage hit 21% HP under a 0.59M Marksmanship spike window. Avenging Wrath had just come out at 1:33, Hammer of Justice was available, but no CC landed on the enemy Mistweaver Monk to prevent healing on the dying Fire Mage. The Paladin's CD toolkit was active but not pointed at the healer. From 1:37 to 1:48 the Fire Mage climbed from 21% back to 57% — the kill window closed without a conversion.

The final kill at 2:16 is nonetheless a win and reflects solid defensive fundamentals: Die by the Sword at 1:18, Blessing of Sacrifice at 1:04, and Exhilaration at 0:56 on the Marksmanship Hunter all landed at appropriate threat thresholds. The improvement available here is specifically healer-CC timing at kill windows: when an enemy target is below 50% HP and a major damage CD is active, Hammer of Justice on the enemy healer — not on the damage dealer — is the tool that converts passive pressure into actual kills before dampening is needed.
