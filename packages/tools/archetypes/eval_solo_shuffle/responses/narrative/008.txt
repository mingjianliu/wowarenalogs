Fast_passive_tunnel rounds average 55 seconds with steady single-target pressure and low burst — but this round at 84 seconds ran significantly longer than the archetype median, giving the Fury Warrior time to use Recklessness twice. The Disc Priest's job in this archetype is to keep the shaman alive through two burst windows on a single Pain Suppression charge, saving the second for the kill attempt. This match shows what happens when opening CC forces both charges before 50 seconds of play.

The opening was structurally damaging: Blinding Light hit all three teammates at 0:30, burning both the shaman and Disc Priest trinkets in a single global. When Intimidating Shout followed at 0:32 landing on all three players simultaneously, the entire team had no escape tools and took free damage from 0:33 through 0:42 while the first Recklessness + Avatar window was active. Pain Suppression at 0:37 with the shaman at 41% was necessary — the shaman was falling under both Fury Warrior melee and Retribution Paladin pressure with the Priest feared. The second Pain Suppression at 0:49 came when the shaman sat at 39% and the enemy Disc Priest's Power Infusion on the Fury Warrior appeared at 0:46. Both charges gone before 50 seconds.

The purgeable Power Infusion on both the Fury Warrior (player 5's buff) and enemy Disc Priest (player 4's buff) appeared at 0:46. Your Enhancement Shaman can purge, and this was a clean opportunity to remove one or both buffs that were amplifying the second burst wave. Removing even one Power Infusion would have reduced the incoming damage enough that the second Pain Suppression might not have been needed until the actual kill attempt at 1:05. The missed purge was directly upstream of the resource depletion.

Power Infusion from your Disc Priest fired at 0:27 — before the match entered its worst phase — boosting throughput through the first Recklessness window. This was the correct use of the throughput cooldown. The Psychic Scream at 1:00 landing on all three enemies at once (players 4, 5, 6) was excellent CC density and bought approximately 5–6 seconds of reduced incoming damage at a moment when the shaman was at 85%. Unfortunately by then both Pain Suppression charges were already spent, so the breathing room from Psychic Scream only delayed rather than solved the problem.

At 1:05 the second Recklessness + Avatar opened with the shaman at 93% — a misleadingly healthy state that dropped to 27% by 1:11 under Avatar-buffed Rampage. The cleanse of Judgment of Justice and Consecration at 1:11 was proactive dispel work that reduced the passive damage load, but with no defensive cooldowns available the shaman fell from 57% at T-3s to dead at 1:23. For future fast_passive_tunnel rounds against Fury Warrior: resist using Pain Suppression during the opening Blinding Light + Intimidating Shout chain unless the target is visibly dying, because the CC will expire and the shaman will recover naturally. The real danger is the Recklessness window after the CC expires, not the CC itself.
