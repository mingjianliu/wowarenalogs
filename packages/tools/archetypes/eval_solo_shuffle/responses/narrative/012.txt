The heavy_burst_tunnel archetype produces repeated burst windows across long rounds averaging 138 seconds, and this 2:06 match with 38% dampening captures that environment precisely. Two separate Fury Warrior bust rotations, a CC-heavy final 20 seconds, and a kill secured at maximum dampening — this is the archetype's most demanding version, and Skeemzin's performance was mostly sound while leaving one clear strategic gap.

The first Critical burst window opened at 0:32 when Dintrax activated Recklessness and Avatar and received Power Infusion from the enemy Priest Liallivia at 0:33. The Shaman crashed to 65% by 0:36 with all four enemy cooldowns live. Skeemzin answered with Pain Suppression at 0:36 and Power Infusion at 0:37 within a single second, which is faster than the archetype's 4.5-second median CD response latency. That rapid commitment was correct. Psychic Scream at 0:39 onto Dintrax then disrupted the warrior's melee uptime during the last seconds of the burst window. However, the enemy Priest Liallivia had a Power Infusion buff active from 0:33 to 0:48 — 15 seconds of enhanced healing during the window when your team was trying to kill the Warrior, who sat at 59% HP at 0:51 with Liallivia still buffed. Stripping that Power Infusion immediately at 0:33 would have meaningfully reduced the enemy's sustain during the kill attempt. This is the match's primary missed purge.

The mid-match lull from 0:44 to 1:24 saw both teams recover. The Hex onto the Beast Mastery Hunter Diapermaxxer at 0:43 was a proactive move with all enemy cooldowns down (Bestial Wrath still 7 seconds out), representing good timing awareness. Ascendance at 0:56 was also well-placed, burning through the midfield when the match was fully stabilized. The second Recklessness and Avatar window activated at 1:24, and this time Skeemzin's Pain Suppression at 1:26 landed immediately at the start of the window rather than 4 seconds in — demonstrably better proactive placement. Hex onto the enemy Priest at 1:26 simultaneously removed their healing, and the Warrior's HP collapsed from 75% to 6% by 1:32 as a result.

The final 20 seconds were the match's most volatile. From 1:48 to 1:52, a cascade of Binding Shot, Freezing Trap, Psychic Scream, and Storm Bolt landed across all three friendly players in sequence. The Shaman dropped to 20% HP at 1:58 and held there for 8 seconds with 38% dampening — well inside kill range. Skeemzin's Psychic Scream at 1:46 onto Dintrax was the only CC they had available to create space, and it worked. The Paladin's Divine Shield at 2:00 when at 26% bought the breathing room to survive, and Hex onto the enemy Priest Liallivia at 2:01 cut off enemy healing for the final 5-second kill. The Warrior died at 2:06 at 0%, Skeemzin at 81%. The round demanded nearly everything available, and it delivered — the principal coaching note remains that stripping the enemy Power Infusion at 0:33 was the one action that could have made the endgame less desperate.
