The cc_tunnel_reactive archetype is defined by tunneled pressure and a healing posture that skews reactive — CD timing tends toward late, cleanse rates are elevated, and the healer is frequently caught managing damage rather than setting the pace. This 1:53 loss illustrates all of that, with the added complication that the Monk's offensive CC was spent in a low-urgency moment, leaving the final kill window uncontested.

The early game was controlled. The Fire Mage's Combustion at 0:28 ignited the first threat window and the Hunter absorbed it well with Bestial Wrath at 0:31 and Exhilaration at 0:32, crashing to 33% at 0:35 before bottoming at 25% at 0:36. Tisza's Life Cocoon at 0:37 provided a protective shell — however, the Hunter had already activated Aspect of the Turtle at 0:36 to immune through the worst hits, meaning the Life Cocoon and Aspect overlapped in their defensive function for one GCD. Ideally Life Cocoon fires 1–2 seconds earlier at 33–35% HP before the Hunter needs to self-immune, allowing Aspect of the Turtle to be banked for a later moment. As-is this is a marginal note: the Hunter survived and recovered to 98% by 0:42, so the approach worked.

The mid-game from 0:44 to 1:20 saw sustained pressure as the Arms Warrior used Avatar at 0:44 and the Marksmanship Hunter activated Trueshot at 0:53. Tisza held the Hunter through spikes at 0:59 (30% HP) and 1:02 (30% HP) without committing Life Cocoon, correctly recognizing that the Hunter's second Exhilaration could bridge those dips. That restraint was the right call and is consistent with cc_tunnel_reactive monks relying on reactivity while conserving major CDs for genuine crises. The match was at 31% dampening by this point but everyone alive.

The decisive error was at 1:22 and 1:26. With the Hunter at 86% HP and the enemy team not in any organized burst sequence, Tisza spent Leg Sweep on the Marksmanship Hunter at 1:22. Four seconds later at 1:26, with the Hunter at 70% HP, Tisza spent Paralysis — and a Freezing Trap landed on Tisza in the same second, removing the Monk from the fight for 6 seconds. Both CC tools gone within one transaction when the Hunter was not in danger. From 1:43 to 1:53 the Hunter then absorbed 0.81M damage — Aimed Shot 208k, Fireball 172k, Rapid Fire 99k — with the Hunter rebounding from 91% at T-10s to dead in the final 5 seconds. A Paralysis or Leg Sweep on the Marksmanship Hunter or Fire Mage during that burst would have directly interrupted one of those three kill shots. The loss is traceable to this CC economy decision more than any healing throughput failure.
