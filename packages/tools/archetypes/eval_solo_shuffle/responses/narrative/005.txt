The heavy_burst_cc_swap archetype features the longest, most demanding rounds, and this 2:39 loss at 50% dampening was a grinding attrition fight with two genuine kill windows that weren't converted before the Fire Mage finally died to pet damage. The Holy Paladin performed above average for the archetype in CD usage and timing, but one missed Hammer of Justice at the right moment cost the round.

The round's three distinct burst phases tell the story. The first (0:22–0:43) opened with a Combustion + Bestial Wrath combo while the Paladin was CC'd; the Fire Mage stabilized through Ice Block at 1:00 after dropping to 18% HP at 1:00, burning their 240-second immunity to survive. The second burst phase (1:19–1:39) featured the Marksmanship Hunter with Trueshot + Explosive Shot driving a 0.60M spike on the Fire Mage and pushing the enemy Marksmanship Hunter to 15% at 1:45 from the team's counter-pressure. The third and final phase (2:08–2:39) saw Trueshot again, the Fire Mage collapsing from 64% to dead in 31 seconds with dampening removing all self-sustain.

The kill the team needed was at 1:44–1:48. The Marksmanship Hunter was at 31% at 1:44, 15% at 1:45, and 16% at 1:47 — a 3-second window at below 20% HP with Bestial Wrath active on the enemy side. The Fire Mage's Dragon's Breath at 1:45 landed on a nil target (missed). Hammer of Justice had a 4-second cooldown remaining per the 1:35 resource snapshot, meaning it came back around 1:39. At 1:44 the Paladin had Hammer of Justice available and the Marksmanship Hunter was the logical target's enemy healer — a Hammer of Justice on the Mistweaver Monk would have locked heals for 4 seconds while the Hunter was at 15% HP. Instead the Hunter climbed from 15% to 42% in 3 seconds from Monk healing, and the window closed.

The final sequence — triple Leg Sweep on all three teammates at 2:32, with the Fire Mage at 4% HP and both trinkets on cooldown — was unwinnable given the setup. Aura Mastery at 2:35 reduced the follow-up, but 50% dampening and zero trinkets meant the Fire Mage's healing was negligible. The lesson: in this archetype, Hammer of Justice on the enemy healer at the first sub-20% kill window takes priority over all other CD considerations. The round goes to 50% dampening if you don't take the kill when it's presented.
