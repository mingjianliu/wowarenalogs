In heavy burst rounds with multiple offensive windows, the Mistweaver's challenge is holding burst-specific CDs for each wave while remaining active offensively. This 2:44 match against Holy Paladin, Marksmanship Hunter, and Beast Mastery Hunter showed correct Life Cocoon usage through three burst windows but ultimately fell to a fourth window where the team's final defensive CC failed to redirect.

Life Cocoon at 0:23 was the first major decision — deployed on the Warrior after Bestial Wrath had already peaked and the Beast Mastery Hunter began its fade. This was a proactive read on who needed coverage, not a reaction to seeing the HP bar drop. Leg Sweep at 0:30 hitting the enemy Holy Paladin while the Beast Mastery Hunter was at 36–30% HP showed offensive instinct: the enemy healer was the obstacle between the team's damage and a kill. The stun bought several seconds of healing gap from the Paladin, and the hunter dropped to 30%. The Monk's trinket was consumed at 0:51 off a Freezing Trap, leaving no CC break for the next 90 seconds — a significant resource loss that shaped the mid-match.

The first critical burst window at 0:52 (Trueshot plus Bestial Wrath, 0.70M damage, Mage falling from 100% to 36% by 1:04) was survived through sustained throughput rather than a major CD, which is correct behavior when Life Cocoon charges need to be conserved for heavier windows. Blinding Light at 1:06 removed the Monk for 4 seconds with trinket unavailable — this is the type of CC gap that characterizes heavy_burst rounds, where the healer simply cannot respond to everything.

Life Cocoon's second use at 2:05 against the third burst window (0.74M damage, Warrior at 28% HP) was the highest-value decision in the match. The Monk correctly withheld the second charge through the second burst window at 1:24 and deployed it at the right moment. Leg Sweep at 2:26 targeting the Hunter rather than the Holy Paladin left the paladin free to heal through the fourth burst window uninterrupted. The mage had already entered Ice Block at 2:36 at 20% HP — redirecting that Leg Sweep to the enemy healer would have created a 4-second healing gap on the paladin side and potentially prevented the kill. The Monk's trinket was spent at 2:41, but with Trueshot's last burst at 2:43 delivering 119k Wailing Arrow and pet damage simultaneously, the window had already closed.
