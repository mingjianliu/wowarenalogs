In fast passive tunnel rounds, damage comes steadily against a single target and matches end before the full CD rotation completes. The Discipline Priest's primary tools are reactive heals, dispels, and Pain Suppression — not offensive engagement. This 1:23 match against Fury Warrior, Retribution Paladin, and enemy Discipline Priest ended in 83 seconds because an early CC cascade burned the team's entire defensive resource pool before the second Recklessness window arrived.

Power Infusion at 0:27 on a teammate during the Fury Warrior's first Recklessness plus Avatar window was an aggressive offensive choice in a round type where defensive resources are already constrained. With Recklessness active at 0:24 and the Shaman about to be pressed by Blinding Light at 0:30, spending Power Infusion to amplify outgoing damage rather than reserving it for a healing role left the priest without a throughput boost option for the late game. The team got 0.60M of damage out of the Shaman's Ascendance at 0:40, which was meaningful — but the Shaman's HP was still at 41% by 0:48, and the priest subsequently used both Pain Suppressions within the same 50-second window.

Pain Suppression at 0:37 was correct and well-timed — the Shaman was at 41% under Blinding Light plus Hammer of Justice plus Intimidating Shout simultaneously. Pain Suppression at 0:49, used a second time as the Shaman returned to 41% HP, was also the right call given the Warrior was still in Avatar. Both charges within 12 seconds of each other is the consequence of the enemy team's opening CC saturation, not a priest error — but it left zero defensive resources for the second Recklessness window that killed the Shaman at 1:23.

The Psychic Scream at 1:00 hitting all three enemies was the highest-value play of the match. With the Shaman at 85% HP and the Priest on Psychic Scream cooldown, the triple-fear gave the team a window to press the enemy Retribution Paladin to 22% HP. The enemy Discipline Priest's Power Infusion at 0:46 on the Fury Warrior was purgeable — neither the Shaman nor the Discipline Priest removed it. In a round where every second of reduced Warrior damage matters, purging that buff before it expired at 1:01 would have slightly compressed the damage taken during the second Recklessness window that ultimately killed the Shaman.
