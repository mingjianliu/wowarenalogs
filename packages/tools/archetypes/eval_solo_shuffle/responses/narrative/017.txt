In these long CC-heavy rounds with multiple structured burst windows, the healer's challenge is sustaining through each wave while holding major defensive tools for the highest-priority moments. The 2:39 match against Marksmanship Hunter, Mistweaver Monk, and Beast Mastery Hunter ended in a loss when the Fire Mage died under combined Leg Sweep CC and Trueshot pressure with 50% dampening.

The opening sequence showed correct priority ordering. Aura Mastery at 0:28 covered the Warrior's drop from 78% to 51% as Bestial Wrath peaked, and Blessing of Protection at 0:33 extended coverage through the CD expiry. Both tools were appropriate given that the Warrior was the first established swap target. When the enemy Marksmanship Hunter opened Trueshot at 0:36 and pivoted to the Fire Mage, the damage landscape shifted entirely — the Mage took 0.74M over the next 15 seconds and reached 18% HP by 1:00. Blessing of Sacrifice at 0:57 and Avenging Wrath at 1:00 both landed after the Mage was already near death, whereas the Mage needed a defensive response closer to 0:48–0:52 when he was at 78–55% and the burst was still building. The paladin's trinket had been consumed early, which removed the break tool, but Wings pressed against the Mage 15–20 seconds earlier would have covered the damage peak rather than the recovery phase.

Divine Shield at 2:03 was the correct deployment of the remaining major defensive. The Mage had just taken 0.80M in 10 seconds and was at 45% HP — the paladin using DS immuno'd through that burst and allowed continued healing output. This demonstrates good situational reading under pressure. Aura Mastery at 2:35 was correctly timed against the third burst window, though the simultaneous Leg Sweep on all three players at 2:32 had already disrupted the team's ability to respond.

The match-ending sequence illustrates the core difficulty of this round type. Three Leg Sweeps at 2:32 on the warrior, mage, and paladin simultaneously, combined with the mage at 4% HP and trinkets on 70–87 second cooldowns, created an irrecoverable state. The paladin ended at 68% HP with cooldowns available — the loss was determined by CC volume in the final 15 seconds rather than healer positioning or CD timing. Managing the first Trueshot window more aggressively remains the clearest point of improvement.
