Match 024 is the most demanding match in this set — 2:06 against a Fury Warrior carrying Recklessness and Avatar who popped both twice, creating two distinct high-burst windows while the Beast Mastery Hunter provided sustained pet damage across the full match. The heavy_burst_tunnel archetype is defined by exactly this structure: long matches, repeated burst windows, tunnel pressure on one target. With dampening reaching 38%, both teams' healing was meaningfully reduced for the final 40 seconds, making every defensive decision in the late game consequential.

The first burst window at 0:32 through 0:52 was the match's highest-danger moment. The enemy used Recklessness, Avatar, Power Infusion from their Disc Priest, and Bestial Wrath simultaneously. The friendly priest responded within 4 seconds: Pain Suppression at 0:36 with the Enhancement Shaman at 71%, Power Infusion at 0:37 to amplify offensive healing, and Psychic Scream at 0:39 scattering the Fury Warrior at 70% HP. This three-CD response in 4 seconds prevented the window from converting into a kill. The Hex at 0:43 on the enemy Beast Mastery Hunter added a fourth crowd control action, neutralizing pet damage during the recovery. The team was back to 100% by 0:44.

The second burst window at 1:24 opened with the enemy Fury Warrior at only 54% HP from sustained pressure. Hex on the enemy Disc Priest at 1:26 immediately neutralized healing, and Pain Suppression at 1:26 covered both the Enhancement Shaman and Ret Paladin as Champion's Spear landed. The enemy Fury Warrior fell to 6% at 1:32 — nearly killed — but the Disc Priest's Psychic Scream on all three friendly players at 1:52 created a chaotic late-game period where the Fury Warrior partially recovered. The Ret Paladin's Divine Shield at 2:00 was the decisive defensive call that bought time while the Enhancement Shaman and priest survived at 21% and 80%, and the final Hex at 2:01 on the enemy Disc Priest with the Fury Warrior at 81% began the last kill sequence. The Fury Warrior died at 2:06.

The archetype's defining trait — highest offensive participation at 74% and fastest CD response at 4,499ms median — is visible in this match. Psychic Scream was used offensively twice, Hex was used three times, Pain Suppression both times responded to active burst rather than pre-emptively, and Power Infusion amplified output rather than being held. The 38% dampening made the late game's 0.75M burst window at 1:43 survivable only because the priest had correctly rationed defensive CDs across the earlier windows. The win was earned through sustained engagement rather than a single decisive moment.
