Heavy_burst_tunnel rounds averaging 138 seconds give Discipline Priests time to use cooldowns with deliberate timing, and this match shows both the benefit and the limit of that approach. Three Recklessness + Avatar windows in 110 seconds outpaced the Pain Suppression cooldown, ending the match with the shaman dying at 1:50 to Rampage (238k), Execute (134k), and Bloodthirst (95k).

The first Recklessness window opened at 0:09 with a Storm Bolt stunning the shaman immediately. The shaman dropped from 95% to 55% within five seconds. Pain Suppression at 0:22 was the correct response once it became clear the shaman was not recovering through personal healing — the Hammer of Justice on the Priest at 0:18 created a 4-second delay that explains the latency. Power Infusion at 0:48 was used proactively as the second Recklessness window opened at 0:49, boosting throughput through what was a 0.55M-in-10-seconds damage spike. Pain Suppression charged again at 0:53 when the shaman sat at 58% and the warrior's cooldowns were still active — both timing decisions here were competent.

The kill window came at 1:31 with the third Recklessness + Avatar. By this point both Pain Suppression charges were on 149s and 156s remaining cooldowns, Power Infusion had been used, and the shaman had no external mitigation. The shaman dropped from 44% to 8% at 1:46 — a 36-percentage-point swing in 15 seconds — and died at 1:50 with the Priest at 100% HP and unable to do anything meaningful. This is the structural ceiling of this archetype: the Priest was not out of position or misplaying, the round simply ran long enough for the enemy to get a third Recklessness cycle before the Priest's tools reset.

The most impactful play in the match was the Psychic Scream at 1:19 on the enemy Disc Priest at a moment when the Fury Warrior had dropped to 33%. This CC on the healer created a genuine kill window — the enemy warrior fell to 17% at 1:17, was at 12% by 1:30. Your hunter's Bestial Wrath at 1:20 compounded the offensive pressure. But the warrior survived at 12% and recovered to 79% by 1:31 before pressing again. In the heavy_burst_tunnel archetype, teams that generate 28.2% unknown CD timing decisions are often in exactly this position: it is not clear whether to use the Psychic Scream on the enemy healer or on the enemy warrior. Here using it on the warrior at 12% instead of the healer would have extended the CC on the damage dealer, potentially completing the kill. Offensive CC on a sub-15% warrior is higher expected value than CC on their healer when the kill is that close.

The two cleanses in this match — Judgment of Justice off the shaman at 1:33 and Divine Hammer at 1:38 — were timely and reduced the passive pressure that was compounding during the third Recklessness window. The missed purge was the enemy Power Infusion on the Fury Warrior at 0:19 (purgeable buff appeared, neither the Disc Priest nor the shaman removed it). Your shaman has Wind Shear and can purge; removing a Power Infusion from the Fury Warrior in a heavy_burst_tunnel round reduces the peak of each Recklessness window and could allow a single Pain Suppression charge to cover a window that would otherwise require two. Building a habit of purging enemy Power Infusion within 3–4 seconds of it appearing is one of the highest-value adjustments for Enhancement Shamans in this matchup.
