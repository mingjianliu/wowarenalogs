Match 023 was a harder version of the fast_passive_tunnel pattern — a 50-second win against a comp that opened with four simultaneous threats in the first 10 seconds. The enemy Fury Warrior used Recklessness and Avatar at 0:06, Storm Bolt stunned the Enhancement Shaman at 0:10, Freezing Trap caught the friendly priest at 0:10, and Bestial Wrath arrived at 0:14. This three-pronged opener — healer neutralized, highest-damage enemy buffed, primary target stunned — is what the archetype's "passive" pressure looks like in practice: not explosive burst setups, but sustained multi-vector pressure that taxes the healer continuously.

The priest's response was clean given the constraints. Pain Suppression came out at 0:18, immediately after the Freezing Trap expired, with the Enhancement Shaman at 35% HP and still taking Bestial Wrath damage. This was essentially the earliest possible action and correctly identified the target in danger. The shaman recovered from 35% at 0:17 to 47% at 0:18 to 66% at 0:21, confirming effective healing behind the suppression. Meanwhile, Ascendance on the shaman contributed offensive pressure and the Ret Paladin's Hammer of Justice at 0:13 partially interrupted the enemy Fury Warrior's rotation. By the time both Power Infusion charges came at 0:26, the team was back to healthy and converting defensive stability into offensive pressure.

The enemy Fury Warrior fell from 59% at 0:25 to 27% at 0:34, recovered partially as the enemy healer was still active, then died at 0:50 with the Enhancement Shaman at only 21%. The Psychic Scream at 0:46 that scattered the enemy Disc Priest and Fury Warrior was the match-closing action — preventing any emergency heal from the enemy healer during the kill window. Champion's Spear at 0:29 created a brief disruption on both the Enhancement Shaman and Ret Paladin, but by that point the friendly team had established damage lead on the Fury Warrior and the result was not in doubt.

The fast_passive_tunnel archetype's high purge rate (1.94/min) is relevant to note: the enemy Power Infusion appeared as purgeable on the Fury Warrior at 0:29 when the enemy was already at 56% HP. This was a lower-value purge opportunity given the proximity to the kill, but in a match that came closer to the edge — with the Enhancement Shaman finishing at only 21% — faster enemy kill speed from a purge would have provided more margin. The match was won cleanly but was tighter than the final score suggests.
