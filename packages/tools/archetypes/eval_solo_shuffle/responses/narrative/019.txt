In heavy burst tunnel rounds, the Discipline Priest's fastest response latency across archetypes means Pain Suppression needs to land within 4–5 seconds of a burst window opening, not after the target reaches critical HP. This 1:50 match against Fury Warrior, Retribution Paladin, and enemy Discipline Priest ended when the Shaman died to the warrior's third Recklessness plus Avatar window after both Pain Suppressions had been spent in the first 54 seconds.

Pain Suppression at 0:22 was a strong early response. Recklessness plus Avatar opened at 0:09 and the Shaman fell from 95% to 55% by 0:12. The priest waited until 0:22 — 13 seconds into the window — before committing, and the Shaman was at 66–74% HP when it landed. The timing aligned with the Warrior still having 13 seconds of Recklessness remaining, which meant Pain Suppression covered the tail of the burst. An earlier deploy at 0:14–0:16 when the Shaman dropped below 60% would have covered more of the peak, but the call was reasonable given the paladin had opened two back-to-back CC in the Priest and Hunter simultaneously.

The missed purge at 0:19 is the clearest improvement in this match. Enemy Discipline Priest Liallivia's Power Infusion appeared on the Fury Warrior as a purgeable buff at 0:19 and remained for 11 seconds. The Enhancement Shaman can purge, and the team's Discipline Priest can dispel magic — neither removed it. Power Infusion amplifies spell power and haste, meaning the Warrior's burst during the opener was artificially inflated by roughly 10–15%. Purging it would have reduced the damage taken in the window that consumed Pain Suppression charge one, potentially allowing the first charge to be held longer or used later.

The second Pain Suppression at 0:53 arrived against Hammer of Justice plus Recklessness overlap — the Shaman was locked and taking damage simultaneously, and the Priest correctly identified the stacked danger. With both charges spent by 0:53 and the third Recklessness window opening at 1:31, the match outcome was structurally determined. The Psychic Scream at 1:19 landing on Liallivia was high-value — it created a brief healing gap during the team's only remaining kill window on the enemy Retribution Paladin at 12–21% HP. The Shaman dying at 1:50 to Rampage (238k), Execute (134k), and Bloodthirst (95k) in the final 10 seconds reflects that no CD was available to answer the third burst window, not that the priest was late or passive.
