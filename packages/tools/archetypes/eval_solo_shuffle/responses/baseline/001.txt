This was a tight 1:53 round that went to 31% dampening, and the loss came down to a failure to protect the Beast Mastery Hunter during the final healer-locked window. The enemy team — Holy Paladin, Fire Mage, Marksmanship Hunter — ran two back-to-back Combustion windows (0:28 and 1:48) and paired them with consistent CC chains on the Mistweaver Monk to prevent healing. The critical structural problem was that the Monk's trinket was burned early at 0:50 on an Intimidation, leaving it on a 111-second cooldown for the Dragon's Breath at 1:08 — a moment when the Fire Mage was at 15% HP and a coordinated counter-push was possible.

The first major crisis hit at 0:27–0:36, when the Hunter dropped from 99% to 26% under an initial Combustion plus sustained Marksmanship pressure while the Paladin was CC'd with Hammer of Justice. The Monk responded by popping Life Cocoon at 0:37 with the Hunter at 25% — a reasonable reactive call — but had already used Exhilaration and Aspect of the Turtle for the Hunter's survival. The team did stabilize fully by 0:42, and Avatar came out at 0:44 which was a sensible investment into a mid-round equity push that got the Fire Mage to 50%.

From 0:59 onward the enemy team re-targeted the Hunter, producing a 0.57M spike starting at 0:59 that dropped the Hunter to 30% by 1:02. The Monk held the Hunter up through this window without spending any major CDs, which was correct. The Leg Sweep on the Marksmanship Hunter at 1:22 was well-timed — used when it came off cooldown with Trueshot active on the enemy side — and the Fire Mage was driven to 24% at 1:37. However, Avenging Wrath came out on the Holy Paladin at 1:36 and the enemy team fully stabilized rather than conceding a kill, suggesting this was the moment to have had Paralysis ready for the Paladin instead.

The decisive window opened at 1:43 with a 0.81M spike on the Hunter, dropping the Hunter to 59% at T-10 before the Monk could respond. Spider Venom and Scorpid Venom were landing on the Monk simultaneously at 1:45–1:48 and both trinkets were on cooldown. The second Combustion fired at 1:48 on the Fire Mage side, and Intimidating Shout went out at 1:49 targeting the Marksmanship Hunter — the right CC target — but it came one global too late: the Hunter was already at 34% at T-3 and dead by 1:53 from a Rapid Fire (208k) and Fireball (172k) combination. With dampening at 31%, the Monk could not bridge the gap between the CC chains.

The key correctable moment was the Monk's Paralysis usage pattern. Paralysis came ready at 1:26 but was immediately followed by a 6-second Freezing Trap on the Monk at the same timestamp, preventing its use. At 1:33 Exhilaration came out on the Hunter again using the second charge, but Paralysis was still on a 38-second cooldown per the resource snapshot at 1:33. A Paralysis on the Holy Paladin earlier in the round — around the 1:06–1:08 window when the Fire Mage was at 15% and the Warrior had Avatar available — could have forced a kill before the enemy team had Avenging Wrath ready. The reactive pattern throughout this round cost the team the offensive initiative when the enemy's HP was actually low enough to convert.
