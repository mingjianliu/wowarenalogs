This was a 1:53 loss where the Beast Mastery Hunter Thräd died at 0% HP — the match's primary tunnel target — while the enemy team's Holy Paladin survived at 68% HP with full healing throughput maintained throughout. The Mistweaver Monk Tisza kept the Hunter alive through four separate burst windows and a relentless CC chain but could not bridge the final 10 seconds of Combustion and Aimed Shot pressure that finished the kill.

The early game was managed well by Tisza. At 0:28 the Fire Mage Zivac activated Combustion while the Hunter dropped steadily from 97% to 26% over 8 seconds (0:28–0:36). The Hunter was self-sustaining through this phase using Bestial Wrath at 0:31 and Exhilaration at 0:32 as the damage mounted, and Aspect of the Turtle at 0:36 to immune through the worst of it. Tisza committed Life Cocoon at 0:37 just as the Hunter reached 25% HP — timing it after the Hunter had already activated Aspect of the Turtle, which raises the question of whether the Life Cocoon would have been better spent 1–2 seconds earlier before the Turtle activated, allowing the Hunter to potentially save Aspect of the Turtle for a later desperate moment. As-is, the Hunter's self-heal chain and Tisza's Life Cocoon overlapped somewhat, which is a minor inefficiency.

The mid-match phase from 0:44 to 1:13 saw the match stabilize and multiple back-and-forth pressure windows. The Arms Warrior activated Avatar at 0:44, the Marksmanship Hunter Avent used Trueshot at 0:53, and by 0:59 the Hunter had taken another spike of 0.57M damage in 10 seconds — dropping to 30% HP at 1:02. Tisza held the Hunter up through all of this, and the log shows the Hunter recovering to 90% by 1:05 before the Warrior's Avatar faded. Tisza correctly saved both Paralysis and Leg Sweep for later in the match — at 1:22 the Monk used Leg Sweep on the enemy Marksmanship Hunter Avent, knocking them down and buying a momentary damage reduction. Paralysis followed at 1:26 — the timing here is notable because a Freezing Trap also landed on Tisza at 1:26, removing the Monk from the fight for 6 seconds with both of the Monk's CC effects just spent.

The final 20 seconds were the killing phase. From 1:43 to 1:53, the Hunter took 0.81M damage in 10 seconds — the highest single burst of the match — while Tisza was simultaneously being CC'd by Scorpid Venom at 1:45, Spider Venom at 1:48, and Intimidating Shout at 1:49 (via the Arms Warrior's own Intimidating Shout connecting to two enemies, not the friendly side). The final 10 seconds saw Aimed Shot (208k), Fireball (172k), and Rapid Fire (99k) land on the Hunter in sequence. The Hunter was at 64% at T-15s, bounced to 91% at T-10s (indicating Tisza was healing through it), then dropped to dead. The T-10s recovery to 91% means Tisza was putting out healing but could not sustain it through the final 5 seconds of combined Combustion fire and Marksmanship output with the Monk under venoms. The loss is attributable to the simultaneous nature of the final CC chain making it impossible to cast through, not a failure of overall healing decision-making.

The key area for improvement is the Leg Sweep and Paralysis timing: using both within seconds of each other at 1:22 and 1:26 when the Hunter was at 78–70% HP — not yet in genuine danger — left the Monk with no disruptive CC when the Hunter plummeted to 40% and lower during the final burst. Saving one of those two CC effects for the 1:43–1:53 kill window could have interrupted Trueshot or Combustion and potentially changed the outcome. Life Cocoon was timed reactively but given how well it covered the T-25% Hunter at 0:37, the question is whether holding it through the 0:59–1:02 dip (Hunter at 30%) was correct — it was, since the Hunter self-recovered with Exhilaration — suggesting Tisza had good overall CD conservation instincts but needed one more CC tool available at the kill moment.
