Match 025 was a 1:50 loss at 31% dampening against a Retribution Paladin, Fury Warrior, and enemy Discipline Priest. The Enhancement Shaman died to Fury Warrior burst — Rampage for 238k, Execute for 134k, Bloodthirst for 95k in the final 10 seconds — during the third Recklessness and Avatar window the enemy Fury Warrior created across the match. The friendly Discipline Priest Skeemzin finished at 100% HP throughout and never used Psychic Scream offensively, suggesting the priest's attention remained entirely reactive across a match that required offensive intervention to create a kill before dampening became a deciding factor.

The match opened with Storm Bolt on the Enhancement Shaman at 0:08 and Recklessness and Avatar on the enemy Fury Warrior at 0:09 — an immediate opener. The Enhancement Shaman dropped from 95% to 57% between 0:09 and 0:12, a 38% HP loss in 3 seconds. The priest used Pain Suppression at 0:22 with the shaman at 74% — a 12-second gap from when the shaman had bottomed out at 55% to 57%. The shaman's Ascendance and the friendly Beast Mastery Hunter's Bestial Wrath at 0:14 responded offensively, and the shaman recovered to 100% by 0:18 before Pain Suppression even landed, meaning the 0:22 Pain Suppression was slightly late relative to the actual danger window. It still served a purpose as the enemy Fury Warrior's Avatar and Recklessness were still active at 0:22.

Power Infusion came at 0:48 — after the enemy's first Recklessness cycle had ended — as a counter to the second Recklessness and Avatar window that opened at 0:49. Both charges were consumed at 0:48. This was a reasonable response given the second enemy burst window was starting and the enemy Disc Priest's Power Infusion was also active. Pain Suppression came again at 0:53 with the shaman at 58% and the enemy Hammer of Justice just landing at 0:53. The second Pain Suppression was timely. Psychic Scream at 1:19 hit the enemy Disc Priest, which was an offensive CC action — the only one of the match — but it landed on the healer alone at 100% HP during a relatively quiet moment rather than during an active burst window.

The third Recklessness and Avatar window at 1:31 was the kill window. The shaman started it at 44% and reached 8% at 1:46 before recovering to 27% then dying at 1:50. The 0.77M burst over 10 seconds at 1:41 was the highest offensive spike of the match. At this point the priest had no Pain Suppression remaining, no Power Infusion remaining, and no Psychic Scream available to scatter the Fury Warrior. The trinket on the Enhancement Shaman had 53 seconds remaining when Champion's Spear hit at 1:24. The match was structurally lost when the third burst window arrived without defensive tools available. The cleaner path was to use Psychic Scream during the second burst window at 0:49 to scatter the Fury Warrior, extending the window where the team's offensive pressure could drive a kill before the third window arrived.
