This 2:16 win went to 41% dampening and was decided by the team's ability to outlast two Combustion cycles and a pair of Bestial Wrath windows before converting a kill on the Fire Mage with severe dampening doing most of the late work. The Holy Paladin performed well under sustained pressure but left some CD efficiency on the table in the middle third of the round that could have shortened the game.

The opening crisis (0:14–0:23) saw the Warrior taking 0.35M in 10 seconds under Combustion while the Paladin was Dragon's Breathed and Polymorphed in rapid succession. Aura Mastery at 0:20 into the Combustion tail was the right call — absorbing magic damage when a second 0.57M spike was incoming on the Marksmanship Hunter at 0:21. The Warrior pressed Avatar at 0:23 at 79% HP, correctly matching their offensive CD to the moment when all personal defensive pressure was subsiding and enemies were at 82–90% HP.

Blessing of Spellwarding at 0:32 on the Marksmanship Hunter — who was at 39% HP — was the round's clearest strong play. The Hunter had been targeted continuously and was below lethal range; Spellwarding bought 6–8 seconds of invulnerability while the Hunter climbed back to 49% at 0:33. Intimidating Shout at 0:34 on the Mistweaver Monk (CC'd the Monk for the entire Spellwarding duration) added meaningful offensive tempo. The Fire Mage reached 42% at 0:45, which represented a genuine kill opportunity, but the Paladin did not add Hammer of Justice on the enemy healer to close it.

The second major crisis came at 1:10–1:20 with the Warrior sliding from 100% to 51%, then recovering to 100% by 1:24. Blessing of Sacrifice at 1:04 pre-empted this window correctly — it was active during the peak pressure. Die by the Sword at 1:18 with the Warrior at 69% was also appropriate. However, Avenging Wrath came out at 1:33 against a second Combustion at 1:30, and this was the round's most consequential CD decision: Avenging Wrath into an active Combustion window with Bestial Wrath 14 seconds away meant the Paladin was spending their signature CD in a reactive frame rather than an offensive one.

The kill at 2:16 was enabled by 41% dampening making the Fire Mage's self-healing irrelevant. The Paladin's Hammer of Justice at 2:05 — locking an enemy for the final minute push — was a good late CC decision. The round could potentially have been shorter if Hammer of Justice had been applied to the Mistweaver Monk around 1:37 when the Fire Mage was at 21% HP, rather than saved. At 41% dampening the late kill was always going to succeed; the earlier opportunities at 0:45 (Fire Mage at 42%) and 1:37 (Fire Mage at 21%) were the rounds where a shorter finish was possible.
