This 2:44 match pitted the team of Mistweaver Monk, Arms Warrior, and Fire Mage against a Holy Paladin, Marksmanship Hunter, and Beast Mastery Hunter. The Fire Mage died at 2:43 under Trueshot pressure with 52% dampening active. The Mistweaver had major defensive CDs available through most of the match and showed proactive play, but the Fire Mage's repeated appearances as the kill target — across four distinct burst windows — eventually overcame the team's resources.

The early game was managed well. The Bestial Wrath at 0:16 combined with early CC on the Monk (Binding Shot plus Freezing Trap) removed the Mistweaver's ability to heal for the opener. Life Cocoon at 0:23 was correctly deployed on the Warrior who had just been CC'd and was taking pressure. Leg Sweep at 0:30 hit the enemy Holy Paladin as a CC tool to extend the offensive window on the Beast Mastery Hunter, and the enemy BM hunter dropped to 30% by 0:35 before recovering through the Holy Paladin's healing. Both team trinkets and the enemy trinkets were consumed before the 1-minute mark, resetting the CC landscape evenly.

The first critical burst window opened at 0:52 with Trueshot plus Bestial Wrath. The Fire Mage took 0.70M damage in 10 seconds and crashed from 100% to 36% by 1:04. The Mistweaver was blinded at 1:06 by Blinding Light for 4 seconds with trinket on 79-second cooldown, which created a healing gap during this critical window. The Mage reached 33% at 1:11 with the Monk's trinket still unavailable. Paralysis at 0:42 had been used offensively — a reasonable choice — but Leg Sweep at 1:23 came during the recovery phase rather than as a defensive response to the burst window, which limited its immediate value.

The second critical window at 1:24 brought Avenging Wrath plus Explosive Shot plus Bestial Wrath simultaneously. The Fire Mage dropped to 36% at 1:24 and held around 46–56% through the window. The Mistweaver's healing kept the Mage alive through this sequence, which was the correct priority. However, at 1:32 Explosive Shot and Bestial Wrath refreshed together and the Monk dropped to 50% HP at 1:35–1:38 while also healing under pressure — a difficult dual-role situation. Life Cocoon's second charge at 2:05 was correctly held for the third burst window, where the Warrior had dropped to 28% and the Mage was taking 0.74M over 10 seconds.

The final kill sequence unfolded at 2:33–2:43. The fourth burst window opened with Bestial Wrath and Trueshot, delivering 0.42M in 10 seconds against the Mage who was already at 20%. The Mage used Ice Block at 2:36 — a final emergency save — and returned at 15–20% HP. Blinding Light at 2:35 removed the Monk from play for 4 seconds, and the Mage died taking 119k Wailing Arrow in the final moments. The Mistweaver finished at 99% HP with all CDs available, which reflects that the Mage's death came from a CC-created gap during Ice Block's expiry rather than exhaustion of the Monk's healing toolkit. Leg Sweep at 2:26 had targeted only the Hunter, missing an opportunity to lock the Holy Paladin out of healing the burst sequence that followed.
