This match went the distance at 1:51 with dampening reaching 31%, and the Discipline Priest's positioning throughout was the central reason the team lost rather than won. When Bestial Wrath and Power Infusion landed on the enemy side at 0:13, the Enhancement Shaman dropped from 100% to 65% in a single second while the priest was still locked down. Despite this, the priest's Power Infusion came out at 0:19 — a reasonable counter to the enemy offensive cooldown window — though the Enhancement Shaman had already been brought to 57% and the Fury Warrior to 78%.

The most consequential healing decision of the match came at 0:56 when Pain Suppression landed on the Enhancement Shaman while the Fury Warrior was sitting at 74% and actively being targeted. By that point Ascendance had been running since 0:34 and faded at 0:49, leaving the shaman vulnerable. The Pain Suppression was used reactively here with Bestial Wrath still 11 seconds away from expiring and both Psychic Scream and Power Infusion on cooldown. The shaman stabilized temporarily but was brought back into danger at 1:10, dropping to 21% HP, where the second Pain Suppression came out — correctly placed this time with the shaman at genuine kill range. Psychic Scream was cast at 0:31 into a non-player target initially before connecting, and again at 1:30 where it successfully feared both the Beast Mastery Hunter and the Ret Paladin, buying meaningful time.

The enemy team's healer lockdown pattern was highly effective throughout. Skeemzin was hit with Freezing Trap at 0:09, Hammer of Justice at 0:22, Scorpid Venom at 0:25, Spider Venom at 0:28, another Freezing Trap at 0:50, Hammer of Justice again at 0:57, Binding Shot at 1:21, Hammer of Justice at 1:34, and a final Blinding Light at 1:49 that locked the priest out of healing for 4 seconds with only 9 seconds left on the trinket cooldown. This chain of stuns and traps across the full match prevented sustained healing output at nearly every critical juncture. The priest used the PvP trinket to break the 0:25 Scorpid Venom, which was a correct decision given the Fury Warrior was at 32% HP and falling.

The kill window that ended the match ran from roughly 1:41 through 1:51. The Enhancement Shaman fell from 44% at 1:41 to dead at 1:51, with the final 10 seconds seeing pet Bestial Wrath contributing 63k, pet Bloodshed 39k, and Divine Hammer 35k. The Fury Warrior was using Avatar at 1:41 and had Avatar available earlier at 0:55 as well, while Bestial Wrath cycled a third time at 1:47. The priest had no Pain Suppression left — both charges had been spent — and the trinket was still on cooldown with 9 seconds remaining when the final Blinding Light landed at 1:49. The key improvement opportunity is purging Power Infusion off the enemy hunter and priest when those buffs appeared at 0:13 through 0:28. The Enhancement Shaman can purge offensively, and allowing Power Infusion to run its full duration on both enemy players while taking 0.71M over 10 seconds on the Fury Warrior was a missed damage-mitigation action.

With no major cooldowns unused and two Pain Suppressions correctly deployed, the priest's performance was not the primary failure point. The match was lost to an enemy CC chain on the healer that was essentially uninterrupted across the full 111 seconds, and to the failure to purge the purgeable Power Infusion buffs that amplified every offensive window the enemy team created.
