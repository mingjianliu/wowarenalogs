This round ended in 35 seconds — one of the fastest possible Solo Shuffle losses — and the Arms Warrior died to a Combustion window with both defensive cooldowns still running. The enemy team executed flawlessly: Dragon's Breath on the Beast Mastery Hunter at 0:17 to reduce peel pressure, then converged on the Warrior with coordinated Marksmanship and Fire Mage damage. By 0:25 the Warrior was at 97% and the Hunter at 70%, which looks stable, but the 0.61M spike in the 10 seconds ending at 0:25 was already in motion.

The Holy Paladin responded quickly — Aura Mastery at 0:20 into Combustion suggests some early awareness — but Aura Mastery's value is primarily against magical sustained damage, not instant execution windows. At 0:26 Avatar came out on the Warrior at 81% HP, which was correct given the pace of incoming damage. Die by the Sword followed at 0:31 when the Warrior was at 55%, also correct. However, both were running simultaneously when Intimidation landed on the Paladin at 0:31 for 2 seconds, creating a 2-second window where the Warrior had no healer access while the Warrior was at 55% and Combustion was expiring.

The Paladin trinket came off Intimidation quickly, but immediately Freezing Trap hit at 0:33 for another 1 second — and the Paladin used their own trinket to break it at 0:33, spending the trinket on a 1-second duration trap while the Warrior was at 36% and still taking 0.61M spike damage. By 0:35 the Warrior was dead at 23% HP (Aimed Shot for 181k, Fireball for 184k, Black Arrow for 74k in the final 10 seconds) with Avatar and Die by the Sword both still active on a dead player.

The Paladin had every major CD available at the moment the round ended: Lay on Hands, Blessing of Protection, Blessing of Spellwarding, Avenging Wrath, Blessing of Sacrifice, and Aura Mastery (just used). This is the defining problem of a 35-second round — no time to deploy the toolkit. Blessing of Sacrifice at 0:29 when the Warrior hit 73% would have shared incoming damage with the Paladin, and the Paladin was sitting at 100% with no pressure. Blessing of Protection or Lay on Hands at 0:31–0:32 when the Warrior dropped to 55%→30% would have ended the kill window. The trinket spend on a 1-second Freezing Trap at 0:33 while the Warrior was at 36% was the incorrect priority — Lay on Hands or BoP at that moment would have saved the round.
