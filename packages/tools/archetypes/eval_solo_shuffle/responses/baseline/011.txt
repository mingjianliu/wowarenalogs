This was a 50-second win against a Fury Warrior / Beast Mastery Hunter / Discipline Priest enemy team, and the Discipline Priest on your side (Liallivia) managed the round's single critical danger window effectively enough to secure a clean kill.

The match opened with the enemy Fury Warrior Dintrax popping both Recklessness and Avatar at 0:06, instantly creating a high-threat window. Within four seconds the enemy team had Storm Bolted your Enhancement Shaman and Freezing Trapped your Discipline Priest simultaneously at 0:10, removing your healer from the fight for 6 seconds right as the Shaman was being crushed. By 0:14 the Shaman was at 76% and dropping 0.47M damage in the first 10-second window, and the Priest was still trapped. Liallivia's Pain Suppression came out at 0:18 — about 4 seconds after the Shaman had already plummeted to 35% HP at 0:17 — which is a reactive response rather than a proactive one. The suppress arrived late enough that your Shaman spent several seconds in genuine kill range. Ideally Pain Suppression would go on the Shaman when the Priest trap broke at 0:16, not two seconds later after the 35% low point was already registered.

Despite the late Pain Suppression, the window was survived. The Priest's trinket usage was also notable: Liallivia was hit by Scorpid Venom at 0:20 and then Spider Venom at 0:23 in quick succession, burning the PvP trinket on the second hit. Meanwhile the Shaman Letsgoflyers had already used their trinket at 0:16 to break out of Psychic Scream. The back half of the match from 0:25 onward was comparatively quiet — both teams were recovering from their respective cooldown dumps — and Liallivia used Power Infusion at 0:26 while the team was stable (all three players between 56–77% HP), which is a reasonable investment window to accelerate damage output.

The killing blow came at 0:50 on the Fury Warrior. At 0:29 the enemy Priest popped Power Infusion on the Warrior (a purgeable buff visible at 0:29), and notably neither the Enhancement Shaman nor Liallivia appears to have stripped it. The Warrior's Power Infusion buff faded naturally at 0:44 — a full 15 seconds of uptime. Purging that buff earlier could have shortened the enemy Warrior's output window meaningfully, especially given that from 0:30 to 0:34 the Warrior's HP was bouncing between 38–56%, and your team was driving the kill that entire time. The Psychic Scream at 0:46 landing on both the enemy Priest and Warrior was the decisive action — it bought enough time for the final 7% of Warrior health to burn away by 0:50.

Heading into the final seconds, Liallivia still had Pain Suppression available (used again noted at 0:50 post-death) and both charges of Power Infusion on cooldown — consistent with short-match behavior where not all cooldowns are required. The key improvement area is the 2-second delay on Pain Suppression during the 0:14–0:16 dangerous window, and the missed opportunity to purge the enemy Warrior's Power Infusion buff at 0:29, which could have reduced how close the match felt in its final phase.
