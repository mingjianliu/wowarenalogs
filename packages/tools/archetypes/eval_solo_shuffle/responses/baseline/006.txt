This round was decided by dampening and Life Cocoon timing, not by raw healing throughput. At 0:52, the enemy popped Trueshot and Bestial Wrath simultaneously — the most dangerous burst window of the game — and the Fire Mage plummeted from 100% to 65% in three seconds. You used your trinket at 0:55 and succeeded in getting the mage back to full within seconds, which was the right call. The problem arrived later: your second Life Cocoon charge wasn't available again until 2:05, and that's exactly when the enemy threw their next Bestial Wrath + Explosive Shot combination at the mage. You used Life Cocoon there correctly at 2:05 when the warrior had already dropped to 32%, but the mage simultaneously fell to 69% and kept sinking. Holding the second Life Cocoon charge instead of using both defensives in the same burst window at 0:23 would have given you more tools late.

The critical kill came at 2:43, when Trueshot fired and the mage died from 57% to dead in under 10 seconds. The mage had been sitting at 15–20% for seven seconds from 2:37 onward while you were Blinded (2:35 Blinding Light, trinket on a 89s cooldown at that point). That seven-second gap with the mage sitting at 15–22% HP and your trinket unavailable was the kill window. The cleanse of Blinding Light freed you, but with the mage at 20% and Trueshot active, the Wailing Arrow (119k) plus pet damage was enough to one-shot the remaining health.

Your Leg Sweep usage was largely sound — 0:30 on the paladin, 1:23 on both hunters, 2:26 on the MM hunter — all during periods where your mage was low and you needed to interrupt incoming pressure. The 1:23 Leg Sweep on both hunters when the mage was at 37% and you had a critical window open was excellent. However, the Leg Sweep at 2:26 hit only the MM hunter while the mage sat at 52–63% with another burst window starting at 2:33. You had Paralysis, Leg Sweep, and Life Cocoon all ready at 2:26 — banking Leg Sweep one more second to use it once Bestial Wrath landed at 2:33 would have given you a shutdown during their actual kill attempt.

Dampening at 52% means the final Trueshot window killed through what would normally be healable damage. But the positioning problem persists regardless: in the last 30 seconds you were CC'd for nearly 10 of them (Blinding Light, Scorpid Venom, Spider Venom, Binding Shot in sequence), and all of your trinket and CC-break resources were exhausted. Against double hunter you need to respect that you will be CC'd during every major enemy cooldown window; saving a Paralysis on the paladin healer earlier to prevent Avenging Wrath from landing cleanly could have bought time, but at 52% dampening the match was realistically very close to a forced outcome.

Going forward against this composition: track when both Bestial Wrath and Trueshot come off cooldown simultaneously, because that is the only window where the mage can actually be killed. Budget your Life Cocoon charges around those two cooldowns specifically. When the mage is below 25% and you are CC'd or about to be CC'd, use Paralysis preemptively on the paladin even if you cannot see the exact damage coming — denying the paladin mobility and cooldown access in that window is more valuable than saving Paralysis for a later offensive stun.
