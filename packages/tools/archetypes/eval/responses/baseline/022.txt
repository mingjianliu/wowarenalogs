This is the miss that gave the enemy their best kill window of the match and forced Pain Suppression at 1:18 on a Ret at 34% instead of as a proactive tool.

Your Power Infusion usage patterns were reasonable on timing but the second use at 2:24 went onto your warrior at 100% HP. That charge is better held for a moment when your warrior is taking pressure at 70–75% and needs the additional survivability and damage output synergy, not spent as an opener at full health.

The missed Landslide dispel at 0:29 was a softer version of the same problem — you were occupied elsewhere, but that debuff sat for 6 seconds on you for only 9k damage, so it was low stakes. The Oppressing Roar misses at 1:08 were not.

Pain Suppression went on at 3:12 on a Ret at 82% — that is early. The kill at 4:16 came after the buff had already faded. Saving Pain Suppression for the 4:06 Avatar window would have been a better allocation.
