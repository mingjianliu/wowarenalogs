This was a 50-second loss where your Windwalker Monk died at 0:22 into a critical offensive window that ran from 0:10 to 0:36. The enemy Arms Warrior opened Avatar immediately, and with your healer Hammer of Justiced at 0:15, the Monk dropped from 86% to dead inside 10 seconds with no defensive answer.

The core problem was Avenging Crusader landing at 0:21 — one second before the Monk died. That CD came too late. When Avatar popped at 0:10 and the Monk started taking 0.55M damage in 10 seconds, that was the moment to pop Avenging Crusader, not 11 seconds later. You had it available the entire window.

You also had Blessing of Sacrifice and Blessing of Protection sitting ready throughout the death sequence. Blessing of Sacrifice on the Monk at 0:12 when he hit 84% would have bought time. Blessing of Protection at any point in the Bladestorm-Mortal Strike finisher at 0:22 would have prevented the kill entirely.

After the Monk died, you played through two more burn attempts on your Druid (0:26, 0:36) and held up reasonably well — the Druid survived to match end at 48%. The healing gaps at 0:34 (3.6s) and 0:40 (10.5s) were unnecessary given your output tools were available; the 10.5-second gap while the Druid was under pressure is the kind of lapse that compounds quickly in a 3v2.

Your team also had no purgers. Power Infusion went out on both the Warrior (0:17) and the Disc Priest (0:17) and sat there for 15 seconds contributing to the Monk's death. You cannot remove it as Holy Paladin, but it's worth noting for comp selection.

Key adjustments:
- Recognize when Avatar fires with a healer CC'd simultaneously — that is your Avenging Crusader trigger, not a fallback option.
- Blessing of Sacrifice belongs on a teammate the moment an offensive CD window opens on them, not after their health is already critical.
- In a 3v2 scenario, stay active. A 10-second healing gap on a player under pressure when your CDs are ready is not sustainable.
