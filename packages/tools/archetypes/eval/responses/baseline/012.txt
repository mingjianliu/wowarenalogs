Good win — your team survived an extremely threatening opening Bloodlust/Ascendance/Avatar triple-CD window and eventually outpaced the enemy healer through repeated burst windows on the Frost Mage. A few things to sharpen.

The opening Bloodlust + Ascendance + Avatar window from 0:09 to 0:33 was the highest-danger moment of the match. The Frost Mage dropped to 30% at 0:40 and 17% by 2:00. Your team's decision to use Shadowfury into the Shaman at 0:15 was correct — interrupting the Shaman's damage flow during Ascendance is exactly right. But the Mortal Coil into the Arms Warrior at 0:21 (at 58% HP with Avatar still active) was a weaker target — the Warrior was mid-Avatar and your coil didn't meaningfully reduce incoming damage on your Mage. Against a three-cooldown window, use your CC on the player actively killing your target, not the one whose threat will fade in 8 seconds anyway.

Dark Pact charges throughout the match were used well as reactive defensive tools for yourself, but watch the timing relative to your Mage's health. At 0:49 you popped Dark Pact while your Mage was already at 54% and falling — the heal from Dark Pact only protects you, not your teammate. The monk used Life Cocoon correctly at 0:53 to absorb the incoming burst on the Mage. Your job in that same window is to CC the enemy's damage dealer, not self-heal. Shadowfury or Mortal Coil on the Arms Warrior in the 0:48–0:52 window would have bought more time than Dark Pact on yourself.

The Shadowfury + Leg Sweep chain at 1:22–1:24 that caught the Warrior and Shaman together was a strong setup. When those two CCs land simultaneously and the Mage is targeting the enemy healer (Evoker at 100% but starting to build), you want to immediately vocalize the swap timing. The 0.74M spike from 1:28 to 1:38 with the Evoker falling from 43% to 30% shows the damage was landing — you needed one more layer of CC to close there.

The Mortal Coil into the Evoker at 2:12 when they were at 14% HP was a perfect execution — using a CC to delay healing while your Mage finished the kill at 2:15 is exactly the right use of that ability. Same logic applied at 2:20 when your Monk immediately followed with Leg Sweep to lock down the remaining enemies and secure the Shaman kill.

Main habit to build: in triple-CD enemy windows, prioritize CC on the player actively connecting damage on your Mage, not off-target fearing. Dark Pact is a personal survivability tool — don't use it as a substitute for CC when your teammate is dying.
