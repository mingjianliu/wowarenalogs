The enemy plan was clear from the first second: Champion's Spear on you and your Rogue to lock your mobility, then Warrior Avatar + Evoker Oppressing Roar into the Mage. They ran it twice and the second time killed her. You spent the first 50 seconds managing the opener and used your most valuable defensive CDs before the game had found its rhythm.

At 0:05 Champion's Spear landed on both you and the Rogue simultaneously — a 6-second root. Standard fast_nuke opener: neutralize the support and healer while the damage rolls in. Your Mage was not CC'd, which means she could move, but the Warrior was already at 100% HP burning Avatar into her. Your first response was Guardian Spirit on the Rogue at 0:07. The Rogue was at 86% HP. You had no reason to believe he was the tunnel target — the Mage was at 97%, the Warrior had Avatar up — and Guardian Spirit on an 86% target 7 seconds into the match is a CD you burned because you panicked, not because you needed to. That charge did not come back until 3 minutes later, which was 1 minute after the match ended.

Psychic Scream at 0:11 hitting the Evoker alone was a good decision. She was in the middle of her healing rotation and the Scream pulled her out for 6 seconds while the Warrior's Avatar was active. Power Infusion at 0:12 went onto the Mage at 78% then yourself — reasonable timing given the incoming damage stack.

The problem arrived at 0:18. You used Divine Hymn. The Mage was at 78%, you were fine, the Rogue was at 78%. The Warrior had Avatar and Intimidating Shout landing — pressure, but not a death scenario. Hymn has a 180-second cooldown. The match ended at 2:01. You cast Hymn at 0:18, which means it came back at 3:18 — 77 seconds after the Mage was dead. At 1:40–1:49 when the Mage was taking 0.98M and 0.81M in two overlapping burst windows, you had no Hymn.

Apotheosis at 0:52 was defensively sound — the Mage had been at 44% under Sleep Walk at 0:45 and needed stabilization. It expired at 1:12. By 1:30 you were a straight Holy Priest with no CD amplification left except Guardian Spirit, which you saved correctly this time.

At 1:30 the Mage was at 15% HP. Guardian Spirit landed immediately and she recovered to 100% at 1:33. This was the right call — 15% HP with the Mage carrying your entire damage output is an emergency. But the save only delayed the timeline: with no Hymn, no Apotheosis, no spare Guardian Spirit, and Power Infusion on a 34-second CD, you had no ability to sustain the second wave.

The second wave arrived at 1:41. Avatar refreshed for the Warrior, the Mage took a 0.81M spike alongside the Rogue's simultaneous 0.81M burst. Intimidating Shout landed on the Rogue for 1 second and the Mage for 6 seconds at 1:44 — the Mage was at 47%, falling, and locked for 6 seconds of the kill window. Power Infusion came off cooldown at approximately 1:51 — 2 seconds after the Mage died at 1:49. Devour from the Demon Hunter hit for 271k, Execute for 224k, Void Ray for 164k. The Mage ended the fight at 33% → dead.

There was one unused tool. Desperate Prayer was never cast in this match. At 1:44 when Intimidating Shout hit you for the second time and you were at 100% HP, casting Desperate Prayer preemptively would have freed you from personal health management and given you one more direct heal on the Mage. She had 5 seconds of being at 47–32% HP while locked. One more cast might not have saved her, but the CD was available and went unused.

Oppressing Roar at 0:07 sat on you and the Rogue for the full 10-second duration. You broke from Champion's Spear at 0:11 — you then had 6 seconds where you could have dispelled Roar off the Rogue (78k damage taken under it). The fact that you cast Mind Control at 0:30 and Guardian Spirit at 0:07 but didn't dispel Roar off your physical carry during the opener reflects where your attention was going.

The core lesson: in a fast_nuke_tunnel game, your defensive CDs are timed against two burst windows, not one. The first window (0:17) is always Avatar + Oppressing Roar. The second window (1:40) is Avatar again with Demon Hunter burst. You need Hymn for window two, not window one. Hold it, lose the first window manageable, win the second.
