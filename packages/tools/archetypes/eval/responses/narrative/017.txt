Heavy burst tunnel matches are defined by their peak burst intensity — teams that execute two or more stacked burst windows in rapid succession. The Mistweaver's job in this archetype is to manage CD layering across multiple peaks, ensuring there is always something held for the second wave after the first is absorbed. This is where the match was lost.

The enemy's opener at 0:10 was structurally overwhelming: Avatar, Oppressing Roar, then Deep Breath at 0:12, all compounded by Champion's Spear rooting both you and your Arms Warrior simultaneously for 6 seconds. This is textbook heavy_burst_tunnel setup — isolate the healer, compress healing windows, stack CDs. Your Mistweaver responded well in isolation: Leg Sweep at 0:11 hit both the enemy Warrior and Enhancement Shaman, Intimidating Shout at 0:14 hit all three enemies, and Life Cocoon came out at 0:19 when you were at 55% and dropping. Your own Ascendance at 0:19 was the emergency call to try to survive.

The structural failure is that both Life Cocoon and Ascendance were spent in the same 2-second window to handle the first burst peak. When the enemy Enhancement Shaman hit Bloodlust and Ascendance simultaneously at 0:27 — the second and heavier burst peak — there was nothing left. The 1.18M on you in 10 seconds starting at 0:27 is not survivable with only standard throughput healing and no emergency tools available. You died at 1:11, and with your Mistweaver Sleep Walked from 1:04 to 1:12 (trinket burned 80 seconds earlier), the Warrior and Monk followed within 7 seconds.

The lesson for heavy_burst_tunnel matches is that CD staggering is as important as CD usage. Ideally the Mistweaver identifies the enemy's secondary burst window (Bloodlust/Ascendance being flagged in this case) and reserves either Life Cocoon or a defensive CC sequence specifically for it. The opener must be survived with only partial tools, accepting damage but staying alive, so that the second window can be answered.

Die by the Sword on your Warrior at 0:28 was a good instinct — he was at 32% — but even with that active the Warrior dropped further before recovering, indicating the overall healing throughput deficit was too great to be solved by any single CD.

Against this specific comp (Enhancement/Evoker/Arms), the Evoker's Oppressing Roar and Deep Breath serve as the CC that enables the burst; neutralizing the Evoker sooner, or getting your Warrior onto the enemy Enhancement Shaman to interrupt Bloodlust, were the only paths to a different outcome.
