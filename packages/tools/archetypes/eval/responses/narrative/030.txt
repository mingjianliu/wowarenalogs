Every time the Shaman applies Flame Shock to the Ret, cleanse it within 3 seconds on your first free GCD. You were doing this for Sleep Walk (correctly prioritized) but not for Flame Shock. The math is simple: one GCD at 0:09 saves 15 seconds of healing pressure. Over five applications that is 75 GCDs of indirect healing you did not have to do.
