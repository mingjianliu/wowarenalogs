This archetype is defined by compressed kill windows — teams that stack their major cooldowns early and try to force a death in the first 30–40 seconds before the healer can respond. Top Preservation Evokers in this cluster leave many cooldowns unused because games end before they're relevant, and offensive CC is sparse. The defining skill is reading the enemy's opener, committing the right defensive tools immediately, and then sustaining pressure offensively once the enemy's burst is spent.
