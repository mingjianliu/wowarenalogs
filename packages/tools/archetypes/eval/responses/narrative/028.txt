You won this match in 1:45 by surviving the most chaotic opening 30 seconds of arena play imaginable and then converting that survival into a clean three-kill chain. The enemy team — Shadow Priest, Arms Warrior, Restoration Shaman — opened with everything simultaneously. By 0:16 you were managing Silence on yourself, Storm Bolt on your Warrior, Champion's Spear on both DPS, Intimidating Shout effects, Power Infusion buffing two enemy players at once, and incoming damage spikes of 0.53M and 0.58M on your Warrior and Paladin in overlapping 10-second windows. That you didn't lose anyone in that sequence was entirely down to two decisions.

First: Nature's Swiftness into Ironbark on the Paladin at 0:22 when the Paladin hit 41% HP. The Paladin was the deepest target at that moment — the Warrior had Die by the Sword up and the Priest's Power Infusion was about to fall off. Ironbark's 20% damage reduction combined with your emergency instant Regrowth through Nature's Swiftness was exactly the right call. You had six seconds until Tree of Life could take over.

Second: Incarnation: Tree of Life at 0:28. The timing was correct — Power Infusion had just faded off both enemy DPS, your Paladin was back to 94% from Ironbark plus heals, and the storm of Cascading Horrors was landing. Tree of Life let you heal through the CC on yourself (Cascading Horrors briefly put you in an incapacitated state at 0:28) while simultaneously putting out enough throughput to keep the Warrior and Paladin full. By 0:39 both DPS were at full health. The crisis was over.

Then your team counterattacked. The Arms Warrior Avatar'd at 0:12, Intimidating Shout onto the Shaman at 0:29 to shut off enemy healing for several seconds, and your DPS focused down the Shadow Priest. The Priest hit 46% at 0:21 during the initial chaos — their own team was generating so much pressure that the Priest was taking friendly fire from the AOE chaos — and by 0:49 the Paladin's spike was building them toward the kill. The Priest died at 0:51.

After the Priest died, the match was won. With the Shadow Priest gone, you had no more Silence threat, no more Vampiric Touch pressure, and the enemy Shaman alone could not heal through two fully functional DPS plus your CC chain. The Shaman died at 1:35 with your Warrior's Avatar up again and the Paladin's Hammer of Justice keeping the Shaman locked. The Arms Warrior died at 1:44.

Your Cyclone at 0:42 was absorbed by a Grounding Totem — worth noting that against Shaman, the first Cyclone attempt will frequently hit a Grounding Totem placed in anticipation. This one didn't matter because the Priest was already burning, but in future matches coordinate a bait or have your Warrior break the totem before you commit the CC. The six missed cleanses (Vampiric Touch, Shadow Word: Madness sitting on players) were partially irrelevant given the 105-second match length — those DoTs simply didn't have enough time to deal meaningful total damage.
