The timing feels safe but it burns the CD during the recovery rather than during the crisis.

The adjustment is simple but requires discipline: in short_tunnel matchups, hold Ironbark until a friendly is below 60% HP and actively dropping. Apply it as an emergency brake on the descent, not as a comfort tool during recovery.
