**The Flame Shock problem**

Five logged missed Flame Shock cleanses: 140k at 0:09, 254k at 0:32, 181k at 1:02, 212k at 1:48, 235k at 2:09. This totals roughly 1.02M in sustained damage on the Ret Paladin from a dispellable dot. That is the equivalent of approximately two full Pain Suppression windows worth of incoming damage that you could have eliminated with GCDs.

The 45% missed cleanse rate for this archetype is reflected exactly here. In heavy_burst_cc_swap, you are intentionally trading off reactive dispelling to maintain your offensive damage output through Penance, Holy Fire, and Atonement. That tradeoff is correct during burst windows when your throughput is generating heals. But Flame Shock is not a burst mechanic — it ticks for 18 seconds at a predictable rate. Dispelling it within 3 seconds of application costs you one GCD and saves approximately 140–254k over the next 15 seconds. The math favors the cleanse unless you are in the middle of a burst window.

The best Flame Shock cleanse in the match was at 3:08: you removed Flame Shock, Earthbind, Permeating Chill, and Capacitor Totem from the Warrior in one GCD, saving roughly 18 seconds of stacked debuff damage. That is the correct execution when you have a free GCD. Build the same reflex for the Ret's Flame Shock earlier in the match.
