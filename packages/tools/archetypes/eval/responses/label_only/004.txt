The archetype label is fast_nuke_swap, but this match did not play out as a fast nuke — it ran 3:34 into dampening. The enemy comp was Affliction Warlock, Frost Mage, Disc Priest — a sustained DOT grind with swap potential, not a fast-kill comp. Your team went in expecting one pace and faced another.

The three-player Curse of Tongues at 0:54 is the match-defining mistake. All three of your players had Curse of Tongues for 28–30 seconds. Curse of Tongues slows casting speed by 50%. As the healer, this means your cast times doubled. During those 28 seconds, every healing spell you cast took roughly twice as long, which is why the enemy's sustained DOT pressure accumulated faster than your throughput could address it. The Evoker can dispel Curses. This was the most important GCD decision in the first minute and it was not taken.

The match then evolved into a duel of cooldown availability. You ran Rewind at 0:37 during a sharp 66% to 34% drop on the Shaman — good decision, necessary survival tool. Emerald Communion at 1:42 was a reset that bought the team back to full. Deep Breath at 0:48 was used defensively. You were spending CDs at the right moments, but the Curse of Tongues tax meant you were always slightly behind on healing output between those CD windows.

The enemy's strategy was to repeatedly CC you — Fear, Polymorph, Howl of Terror, Psychic Scream — so that the DOTs on your DPS accumulated while you were locked out. You handled most of these well: Sleep Walk on the Disc Priest at 0:55 and 1:00 was good disruption. The back end of the match, from 3:00 onward, saw the enemy pivot to killing you directly with three consecutive burst windows.

In fast_nuke_swap matches against sustained comps, the healer survival answer is: stay mobile, use Obsidian Scales to absorb burst, and dispel Curses the moment they land. You executed the first two. The Curse dispel failure in the first 30 seconds created a healing deficit that the rest of the match required constant CD expenditure to compensate for.
