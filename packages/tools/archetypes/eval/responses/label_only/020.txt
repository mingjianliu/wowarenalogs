With all three of you incapacitated under Leg Sweep immediately after, you had no opportunity to capitalize on that HP deficit. The last two Screams at 3:15 and 3:44 were mopping up — Monk at 3:15, then Monk+Evoker at 3:44 after the Ret was already dead. Those closed the game correctly.

**What to change**

Three specific adjustments:
1. Resolve your target selection before casting Guardian Spirit — especially in fights where pets are on-screen. The Water Elemental misclick was avoidable.
2. In a cc_heavy_swap match, your Rogue being Frozen or Blinded is always high-priority cleanse, above most other targets. Physical carry CC loses go windows.
3. When you identify the primary kill target (in this match, the Ret), coordinate your Psychic Scream timing to land on the Mistweaver during the Rogue's Kidney Shot, not seconds before or after. The 1:22 Scream was the best example of this.
